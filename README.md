## [04-hebbian-steering](https://ccli3896.github.io/dendrite/04-hebbian-steering/)
Live, in-browser simulations of reward-seeking behavior emerging from local Hebbian-like plasticity, comparing seven local learning rules (PC, BCM, Oja, Law & Cooper, covariance, LPL, RPE): passive responses to the same input, reward seeking, learning through neuron turnover, continual learning (Hebbian vs. three-factor RPE rule), open-field search, and 3D attractor views of the deterministic and matching-pennies tasks. Built as talk animations.

From paper, [*Reward-seeking behavior emerges from Hebbian-like plasticity*](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.11.18.767638v1) by Li, Brenner, Ye, Du Pont, Kudarauskas, Chen, & Erlich 2026. The theory of why it works is in the companion paper, [*Prospective configuration learns by drift and diffusion along line attractors in closed-loop environments*](https://github.com/ccli3896/hebbian-flexible-attractors/blob/main/companion_paper/Li_Erlich_2026_prospective_configuration.pdf) by Li & Erlich 2026. The open-field demo uses the model from [*Neuron-level prediction and noise can implement flexible reward-seeking behavior*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11142161/) by Li, Brenner, Boesky, Ramanathan, & Kreiman 2024.
